03 Technical skills Full skills matrix ↗

What I work with

Four areas I keep returning to — the ones I'd be comfortable handing to someone else, not a list of everything I've ever touched.

01

Robotic manipulators & kinematics

Closed-form inverse kinematics, DH parameters, spherical wrist geometry, and cycloidal reducers designed to take backlash out of a joint under load.

ROS2 MoveIt 6-DOF Cycloidal drives
02

BLDC motor control & FOC

SVPWM, Clarke and Park transforms, shunt current sensing, and cascaded current–velocity–position loops for haptic and dynamic torque control.

STM32F4 25 kHz SVPWM 14-bit encoders
03

Embedded systems & distributed CAN

FreeRTOS task design, DMA timer-synchronised conversions, isolated CAN transceivers, and low-latency ESP-NOW links for steer-by-wire and swarm agents.

FreeRTOS CAN 2.0B ESP32-S3
04

CAD & digital fabrication

Parametric SolidWorks models with FEA checks, 3-axis CNC milling of aluminium, GD&T drawings, and carbon-nylon prints for structural parts.

SolidWorks CSWA CNC 6061-T6 PA-CF printing

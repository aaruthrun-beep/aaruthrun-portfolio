I build robots — the mechanical parts, the motor controllers, and the firmware that makes them move. Most of what I know came from taking things apart and putting them back together a little better.

I'm studying Robotics & Automation Engineering at PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, after a diploma in Mechatronics. My bench is usually mid-surgery: a 3D-printed six-axis arm, a BLDC actuator that has to hold position, or a rover learning to find its way across a room.

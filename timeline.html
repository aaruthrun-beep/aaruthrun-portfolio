PARALLEL R&D SYNERGY

Cross-Pollination of Technology Across Concurrent Tracks

Building physical robots is inherently parallel: while cycloidal drives for NITE 369 were undergoing CNC machining and dial-gauge measurement, the 25 kHz FOC brushless control algorithms were simultaneously derived on motor test benches. Concurrently, the Titan-Klipper CoreXY machine was fabricated to 3D print carbon-fiber nylon (PA-CF) structural parts for both NITE 369 and MARS 369.
